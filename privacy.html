🏀 Hoop Shot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: this version of the app.

Hoop Shot is a basketball game made for families. We care about your privacy, and the short version is simple: the app does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Hoop Shot does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal data. There are no accounts and no sign‑in.

Data stored on your device

The game saves a few things only on your own device so it remembers your progress — for example, your coin total and the name on the jersey. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Internet, ads, and tracking

Hoop Shot works fully offline and makes no internet connections. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. There are no real‑money purchases — the coins in the game are pretend and cannot be bought with real money.

Children

Hoop Shot is family‑friendly and does not knowingly collect any information from children (or anyone else).

Contact
