⬅ Arcade

⛏️ MO BLOCKS

Dig, collect blocks, and build!

Joystick: walk & jump.
Hold toward a block to mine it (it goes in your bag).
Drag a block from your bag to build with it. 🧱
Mine the leaf bushes 🌿 and lay down soft carpet 🧶!
Find the shiny gems 💎!

🐷 Walk to the friendly pigs at the farm — they share food. Mo eats on his own to fill a heart. ❤️

🏠 Hold the joystick still on the ground to build a quick house. 🌙 At night, spooky zombies come out — hop on one to boop it away! ☀️ They melt at sunrise.

Pick a mode:

🛡️ Survival — hearts, pigs, night zombies.  ✨ Creative — fly, dig instantly, build with endless blocks, no danger.
