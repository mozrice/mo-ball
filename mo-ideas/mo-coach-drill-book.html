🏀 Chapter 1 — Ball Handling & Dribbling

10 drills

1Pound Dribbles

Dribble the ball hard and low, 20 times with your right hand, then 20 with your left. Eyes up the whole time.

Coach tip: Fingertips, not the palm. The harder you pound, the more control you get.

2Crossover Taps

Bounce the ball from your right hand to your left and back, low in front of your knees. Go 30 seconds.

Coach tip: Keep it below your knees so no one can steal it.

3Figure Eights

Weave the ball between your legs in a figure-eight, slow and smooth. 10 times each direction.

Coach tip: Start slow. Smooth beats fast.

4Spider Dribble

Tap the ball quickly — front-front, back-back — between your legs. Count how many taps in 20 seconds.

Coach tip: Tiny quick taps with your fingertips.

5Wall Taps

Stand close to a wall and tap the ball against it with your fingertips, fast, for 30 seconds.

Coach tip: This builds strong, quick fingers for handling.

6Two-Ball Dribble

Dribble two balls at once, both hands pounding together, then take turns (right up, left down).

Coach tip: Keep your head UP — feel the balls, don't watch them.

7Zig-Zag Dribble

Dribble in a zig-zag down the court, crossing over each time you change direction.

Coach tip: Push off your outside foot when you change directions.

8Stop and Go

Dribble fast, freeze on 'STOP', then explode again on 'GO'.

Coach tip: Low and balanced when you stop so you're ready to move.

9Around the World

Circle the ball around your waist, then your knees, then your ankles, without dropping it.

Coach tip: Fast hands! Then go the other way.

10Cone Weave

Set up cones (or shoes) in a line and dribble in and out of them with control.

Coach tip: Change hands at every cone.

🤝 Chapter 2 — Passing

10 drills

1Chest Pass Partners

Face a partner and chest-pass back and forth. Step into every pass. Do 20 clean passes.

Coach tip: Thumbs point down when the ball leaves your hands — that's a good chest pass.

2Bounce Pass

Bounce-pass to a partner so the ball bounces once, about two-thirds of the way to them.

Coach tip: Aim the bounce, not the person.

3Pass and Move

Pass to a partner, then sprint to a new spot. They pass it back. Never stand still.

Coach tip: Good players move right after they pass.

4Wall Passing

Chest-pass against a wall and catch it back, fast, for 30 seconds.

Coach tip: Great when you're practicing alone.

5Find the Open One

Three players stand in a line, one raises a hand. Pass to the open hand, then a new one opens.

Coach tip: Look before you pass — pass to the one who's ready.

6Monkey in the Middle

Two passers, one defender in the middle. Keep the ball away with good passes.

Coach tip: Fake one way, pass the other.

7Overhead Outlet

Grab a rebound and make a strong two-hand pass over your head to a partner up the court.

Coach tip: Turn and look up the floor first.

8No-Look Practice

Look one way and pass another to a partner (only when you've mastered the basics!).

Coach tip: Learn the normal pass first — fancy comes later.

9Star Passing

Five players in a star shape passing across in a pattern. Keep it smooth and quick.

Coach tip: Call your teammate's name before you pass.

10Give and Go

Pass to a partner, cut to the hoop, get it back, and finish.

Coach tip: Speed up right after you pass — that's the 'go'.

🎯 Chapter 3 — Shooting

10 drills

1Form Shooting

Stand close to the hoop and shoot one-handed, focusing on perfect form. Make 10.

Coach tip: Remember B.E.E.F: Balance, Eyes, Elbow in, Follow through.

2Follow-Through Waves

After every shot, hold your follow-through and 'wave goodbye' to the ball until it lands.

Coach tip: A good wave means a good shot is on the way.

3Around the Key

Shoot from 5 spots around the paint. Make one before you move to the next spot.

Coach tip: Square your shoulders to the rim at every spot.

4Free Throw Routine

Do the same thing every free throw: bounce, breathe, shoot. Take 10.

Coach tip: Same routine every time builds a calm, steady shot.

5Beat the Coach

Shoot from a spot; a make is 1 point for you, a miss is 1 for the coach. First to 5.

Coach tip: Slow down and aim — points beat rushing.

6Catch and Shoot

A partner passes to you and you shoot right away in one motion.

Coach tip: Have your hands ready BEFORE the ball gets there.

7Bank Shots

Practice shooting off the backboard square from the side angles. Make 5 each side.

Coach tip: Aim for the top corner of the little square.

821 (Knockout)

Line up and shoot; if the person behind you makes it before you, you're out. Fun with a group.

Coach tip: Rebound your OWN miss fast and put it back up.

9Spot Shooting Ladder

Make 2 from spot 1, then 3 from spot 2, then 4 from spot 3.

Coach tip: If you miss, stay until you get the count.

10Buzzer Beaters

Coach counts '3-2-1' — catch and shoot before the buzzer. Pretend it's the big game!

Coach tip: Quick feet, calm hands.

🙌 Chapter 4 — Layups & Finishing

10 drills

1Right-Side Layups

Dribble in from the right, step right-left, and lay it soft off the square. Make 5.

Coach tip: High knee on the jump.

2Left-Side Layups

Same from the left side, stepping left-right. Make 5. Lefties matter!

Coach tip: Use your left hand — practice makes it easy.

3Mikan Drill

Stand under the hoop and lay it in with your right, catch, lay it with your left, back and forth.

Coach tip: Soft touch and quick feet — a classic!

4Speed Layups

Dribble in fast and finish under control. Don't let speed mess up your touch.

Coach tip: Fast to the rim, gentle at the rim.

5Power Layups

Jump off TWO feet and go up strong through contact.

Coach tip: Be strong and balanced — protect the ball.

6Reverse Layups

Go under the hoop and finish on the other side, away from the defender.

Coach tip: Use the rim to hide the ball.

7Layup Lines

Two lines: one shoots layups, one rebounds and passes out. Keep it moving.

Coach tip: Say 'ball!' when you catch the rebound.

8Finish Through Contact

A coach gives a soft bump with a pad while you finish the layup.

Coach tip: Stay strong and keep your eyes on the target.

9Floater Practice

From a few steps out, float a soft shot up and over. Great for little players vs. tall ones.

Coach tip: High and soft, off your fingertips.

10Coast to Coast

Grab a rebound, dribble the length of the court, finish a layup.

Coach tip: Control the ball, then explode to the rim.

🛡️ Chapter 5 — Defense

10 drills

1Defensive Slides

Stay low, feet wide, and slide side to side without crossing your feet. 30 seconds.

Coach tip: Push and slide — never hop, never cross.

2Mirror Drill

Face a partner; they move slow, you stay right in front like a mirror.

Coach tip: Watch their belly button, not their fancy moves.

3Close Out

Sprint to a shooter, then chop your feet and get your hands up under control.

Coach tip: Don't fly by — break down and be balanced.

4Deny the Pass

Stand between your player and the ball with a hand in the passing lane.

Coach tip: See your player AND the ball at the same time.

5Steal the Cookie

Attacker dribbles; defender times a clean poke at the ball (no reaching fouls).

Coach tip: Take the ball when it's low, not the arm.

6Box Out

When a shot goes up, turn, find your player, and put your back into them to grab the rebound.

Coach tip: Hit first, then go get the ball.

71-on-1 Stops

Play one-on-one; the defender wins by getting 3 stops before the attacker scores twice.

Coach tip: Stay low the WHOLE time — tired defenders reach and foul.

8Take the Charge (soft)

Practice stepping in front and falling safely onto a mat when the attacker drives.

Coach tip: Feet set, arms crossed, take it on the chest.

9Zig-Zag Defense

Slide with a dribbler as they zig-zag down the court, staying in front.

Coach tip: Beat them to the spot.

10Help and Recover

Slide over to help a teammate, then hurry back to your own player.

Coach tip: Talk! Yell 'help!' so your team knows.

💪 Chapter 6 — Rebounding & Hustle

8 drills

1Grab and Chin

Catch a rebound and pull it tight under your chin with elbows out.

Coach tip: Never let the ball dangle low where it gets stolen.

2Tip Drill

Tip the ball off the backboard to yourself a few times, then grab it strong.

Coach tip: Soft fingertips on the tips.

3Two-Hand Grab

Coach tosses the ball off the rim; you jump and grab it with TWO hands.

Coach tip: Two hands are stronger than one.

4Loose Ball Dive

On 'GO', hustle after a rolling ball and grab it before your partner.

Coach tip: First to the floor usually gets the ball.

5Rebound and Outlet

Grab the board, turn to the sideline, and make a strong outlet pass.

Coach tip: Look up the court the moment you land.

6Superman

Toss the ball out, run and grab it in the air, land in balance.

Coach tip: Big effort — rebounds are about want-to.

7Circle the Rim

Two players tap the ball back and forth off the backboard, staying up strong.

Coach tip: Quick jumps, don't come all the way down.

8Box Out Battle

On the shot, box out your partner for 3 seconds so they can't get the ball.

Coach tip: Feel where they are with your back.

👟 Chapter 7 — Footwork & Moves

8 drills

1Jump Stop

Dribble, then land on both feet at the same time in balance.

Coach tip: Stick the landing like a gymnast.

2Pivot Practice

Keep one foot planted and swing the other around to face different ways.

Coach tip: Stay low so a pivot doesn't wobble.

3Triple Threat

Catch the ball ready to shoot, pass, OR dribble — knees bent, ball by your hip.

Coach tip: Be a threat to do all three.

4Jab Step

Fake a step one way to make the defender lean, then go the other way.

Coach tip: Sell the fake with your eyes.

5Crossover Move

Dribble at a cone, cross the ball low, and change direction fast.

Coach tip: Low and quick — the crossover is a whisper, not a shout.

6V-Cut

Walk your defender in, then cut out hard to get open in a V shape.

Coach tip: Change speed — slow, then FAST.

7Drop Step

In the post, feel the defender and step through them toward the hoop.

Coach tip: Big strong step, ball protected.

8Euro-Step (older kids)

Take two steps in different directions to slide past a defender to the rim.

Coach tip: Learn layups first, this comes later.

🔥 Chapter 8 — Conditioning & Warm-Ups

8 drills

1Line Touches

Sprint and touch each line on the court, coming back each time (suicides — but fun!).

Coach tip: Bend and touch the line with your hand.

2High Knees

Run in place bringing knees up high for 20 seconds.

Coach tip: Pump your arms too.

3Defensive Slide Lengths

Slide from sideline to sideline in your defensive stance, 4 times.

Coach tip: Stay low the whole way — no standing up.

4Star Jumps

10 big jumping jacks that turn into star shapes in the air.

Coach tip: Explode up, land soft.

5Dribble Sprints

Dribble as fast as you can under control from one end to the other.

Coach tip: Speed with control, not speed with chaos.

6Lane Slides

Slide around the paint (the key) staying low, both directions.

Coach tip: Quiet, quick feet.

7Arm Circles

10 forward, 10 backward to loosen your shoulders before shooting.

Coach tip: Warm shoulders shoot better.

8Cool-Down Stretch

After practice, gently stretch legs and arms and take slow breaths.

Coach tip: Never skip the cool-down — it keeps you healthy.

🎉 Chapter 9 — Fun Team Games

8 drills

1Knockout

Two balls, everyone in line. Make your shot before the person behind you or you're out.

Coach tip: Grab your miss and put it back FAST.

2Sharks and Minnows

Everyone dribbles across; 'sharks' try to knock balls away without fouling.

Coach tip: Keep the ball low and protected.

3Dribble Tag

Everyone dribbles while a tagger (also dribbling) tries to tag them.

Coach tip: You can't stop dribbling to get away!

4Relay Races

Teams race doing dribble-to-the-cone-and-layup, then tag the next player.

Coach tip: Cheer for your teammates!

5Around the World

Make shots from spots around the key in order. First all the way around wins.

Coach tip: Slow down and aim at each spot.

6Steal the Bacon

Two teams; coach calls a number, those players race for the ball in the middle.

Coach tip: Explode on your number.

7Musical Basketballs

Dribble around; when the music stops, freeze in triple threat.

Coach tip: Freeze low and balanced.

8Beat the Clock

As a team, make 20 layups before the timer runs out.

Coach tip: Work together, keep the line moving.

⭐ Chapter 10 — Team Play & Being a Good Teammate

8 drills

1Spacing

Spread out so the floor is big — don't all run to the ball.

Coach tip: Give your teammate room to make a move.

2Pass Ahead

When you get a rebound, look to pass up the court to a running teammate.

Coach tip: The pass is faster than the dribble.

3Setting a Screen

Stand still and strong to block a defender so your teammate gets open.

Coach tip: Set your feet — don't move once you're there.

4Talk on Defense

Yell 'ball!', 'help!', 'shot!' so everyone knows what's happening.

Coach tip: The loudest team is usually the best defense.

5Cheer From the Bench

When you're resting, cheer loud for your team.

Coach tip: Great teammates cheer as hard as they play.

6Shake Hands

Say 'good game' and shake hands, win or lose.

Coach tip: Winning is fun, but being kind matters more.

7Hustle Back

When the other team gets the ball, sprint back on defense.

Coach tip: First one back stops the easy basket.

8Do Your Job

Play YOUR position and trust your teammates to play theirs.

Coach tip: A team of helpers beats a team of heroes.
