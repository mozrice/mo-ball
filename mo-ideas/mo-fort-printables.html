MO Fort

Printable Fort Pack — 15 parts to print, color and cut out to build a paper fort!

Press Print (Ctrl/Cmd + P). Dashes ✂ = cut. Blue dashes = fold. Tape the tabs to stand pieces up!

1 Fort Front Wall

✎ Color the bricks · ✂ cut · fold the blue tabs and stand it up!

2 Fort Side Wall (make 2!)

✎ Color · ✂ cut · fold the tab

3 Corner Tower (make 2!)

✎ Color · ✂ cut · roll into a tube and tape, or stand flat

4 Tower Roof (make 2!)

✂ Cut the circle · cut the slit · curl into a cone and tape

5 Gate & Drawbridge

✎ Color the wood · ✂ cut · tape the top so it opens down

6 Fort Flag (make lots!)

✎ Color · ✂ cut · tape to a straw or stick

7 MO FORT Sign

✎ Color · ✂ cut · hang it on the gate

8 Knight Mo (the guard)

✎ Color the brave guard!

9 Your Shield

✎ Draw your own crest & color · ✂ cut out

10 Cannon

✎ Color · ✂ cut · stand it by the gate

11 Bunting Banner

✎ Color the flags · ✂ cut · tie on a string across the fort

12 Treasure Chest

✎ Color the gold · ✂ cut out

13 Windows & Arrow Slits

✎ Color · ✂ cut · tape onto your walls & towers

14 Fort Pass (show it at the gate!)

✂ Cut me out · write your name

15 Fort Map

✎ Color your map · mark where the treasure is with an X!
