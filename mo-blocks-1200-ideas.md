# MO BLOCKS — 1200 Ideas (Block Survival, Mining, Crafting & Building)

Ideas for MO BLOCKS, Mo's block-world game, grounded in real block-survival play: exploring biomes, mining ores, crafting tools, surviving the night, farming, taming animals, redstone machines, and big builds. Kid-friendly and gentle: spooky night mobs like zombies are never gory and always fade at sunrise; no magic and no scary stuff. Animals are cared for, and Arff (girl dog) and Meow (cat) are always safe and loved. (An original game inspired by block-survival games — no copyrighted names.)
